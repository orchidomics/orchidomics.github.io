Principal Investigator

L. Alexander Damián-Parizaca, Ph.D.

Alexander is a plant evolutionary biologist who works on the systematics, phylogenomics and biogeography of Neotropical orchids, especially Vanilla. He is Research Director of the Center for Amazonian Studies and Biodiversity Research (CIEAB) at Universidad Tecnológica del Perú in Iquitos and a RENACYT Level II researcher (CONCYTEC). He has 47 publications, 32 of them as first author.

  • Research Director, CIEAB, Universidad Tecnológica del Perú, Iquitos (2026–present)
  • Visiting Professor, School of Environmental Engineering and Agronomy, Universidad Científica del Sur, Lima (2026–present)
  • Associate Researcher, Inkaterra Asociación (2023–2026)
  • Associate Researcher (2016–2023) and Full Professor (2016–2020), Universidad Científica del Sur, Lima
