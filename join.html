🌿 Call for thesis students (2026–2027)

Eligibility: the 2026–2027 call is open to Peruvian students only.

Are you interested in plant biology, botany, orchids, bioinformatics, evolution, herbaria, programming, or fieldwork? Are you looking for a place to carry out your undergraduate or graduate thesis? Fill out the application form to apply as a student in the lab.

Apply now

Requirements

  • Undergraduates: preferably fourth- or fifth-year students.
  • Demonstrable prior experience in at least one of the lab's research lines (courses, workshops, conferences, independent projects, or others).
  • Experience searching the scientific literature (required).
  • Familiarity with online databases such as Tropicos, POWO, or GBIF (desirable).
  • Intermediate English (required): enough to search the literature and to read, understand, and present papers in the field.
  • Experience with programming languages such as R or Python (required for master's and doctoral students).
  • Funding, or a clear funding plan.

What we offer

  • Training in programming (R, bash, and others).
  • Training in bioinformatics: phylogenetic and phylogenomic pipelines.
  • Training in orchid taxonomy, nomenclature, and morphology.
  • Herbarium work and use of online herbarium databases.
  • Training in scientific writing.
  • Opportunities to collaborate on scientific papers.
  • Opportunities to apply, as a thesis student, for national competitive funding such as PROCIENCIA, and for the lab's internal funds.
