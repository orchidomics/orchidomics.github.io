Genomic resources

Genomic datasets and genetic resources from our Vanilla research.

  • Complete plastid (chloroplast) genomes of Vanilla spp.

    Plastid genomes of Peruvian Vanilla species, used for comparative and phylogenetic analyses of the genus (Zuta-Puscan et al. 2026). Preprint

  • Phylogenomic dataset of Vanilla

    Genomic data behind the phylogenomic and historical biogeographic analysis of Vanilla across its range (Damián-Parizaca et al. 2026). Preprint

  • DNA barcodes of native Peruvian Vanilla

    Molecular barcode data for multiple native Peruvian Vanilla taxa, from the FONDECYT project on the genetic and morphological diversity of Vanilla in Peru, in collaboration with Universidad Veracruzana, Mexico.

  • Living collection of Peruvian Vanilla

    More than 100 living Vanilla plants representing multiple Peruvian species, kept at the Inkaterra field laboratory as a reference for research on wild relatives of the vanilla crop.
